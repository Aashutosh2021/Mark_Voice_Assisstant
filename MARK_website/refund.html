Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 27, 2025

1. Refund Eligibility

MARK AI offers refunds under specific circumstances to ensure customer satisfaction while protecting our intellectual property.

2-Day Refund Window

All refund requests must be submitted within 2 days of your initial purchase to be eligible for consideration.

Eligible Refund Scenarios

  • Technical Issues: Software fails to install or activate properly on supported systems
  • Feature Incompatibility: Advertised features don't work as described on your system
  • Payment Errors: Duplicate charges or billing mistakes
  • Pre-delivery Cancellation: Request made before software download/activation

2. Non-Refundable Scenarios

The following situations are not eligible for refunds:

  • Software has been successfully activated and used for more than 7 days
  • User system does not meet minimum requirements (specified before purchase)
  • Change of mind or buyer's remorse
  • Inability to use due to lack of technical knowledge
  • Third-party integration issues beyond our control
  • Hardware compatibility issues with unsupported devices

3. Refund Process

Step 1: Contact Support

Before requesting a refund, please contact our support team for assistance:

  • WhatsApp: +91 9798022573 (Fastest response)
  • Email: support@markai.com

Step 2: Troubleshooting Attempt

Our team will work with you to resolve any technical issues. Many problems can be solved without requiring a refund.

Step 3: Refund Request Submission

If the issue cannot be resolved, submit a formal refund request including:

  • Order number and payment details
  • Detailed description of the issue
  • System specifications and error logs (if applicable)
  • Screenshots or video evidence of the problem

4. Processing Time and Method

Review Period

  • Standard Cases: 3-5 business days
  • Complex Cases: 7-10 business days
  • Technical Investigation Required: Up to 14 business days

Refund Methods

Approved refunds will be processed using the same payment method used for the original purchase:

  • UPI/Digital Wallets: Instant to 24 hours
  • Credit/Debit Cards: 3-7 business days
  • Net Banking: 2-5 business days

Processing Note

Refund processing times may vary based on your bank or payment provider. MARK AI initiates refunds immediately upon approval, but the final credit timing depends on your financial institution.

5. Edition-Specific Policies

Core Edition

  • Standard 7-day refund window
  • Full refund for technical issues
  • Community support assistance required before refund consideration

Pro Edition

  • Enhanced 10-day refund window
  • Priority support assistance included
  • Partial refunds considered for feature-specific issues

Ultra Edition

  • Extended 14-day refund window
  • Dedicated technical consultation before refund
  • Alternative solution options (downgrades, credits)

6. Partial Refunds and Alternatives

In certain cases, we may offer alternatives to full refunds:

  • Edition Downgrade: Refund the difference between editions
  • Store Credit: Credit for future purchases or upgrades
  • Extended Support: Additional technical assistance
  • Feature Credits: Access to specific premium features

7. Dispute Resolution

Internal Resolution

We strive to resolve all refund disputes internally through direct communication with our support team.

Payment Gateway Disputes

If you've initiated a chargeback or dispute through your payment provider:

  • Please contact us first to resolve the issue directly
  • Chargebacks may result in account suspension
  • We will provide necessary documentation to payment providers

Legal Compliance

This refund policy complies with Indian consumer protection laws and Razorpay's refund guidelines.

8. Special Circumstances

Medical/Emergency Situations

We may consider refunds outside the standard window for documented medical emergencies or exceptional circumstances.

Force Majeure

In cases of natural disasters, global pandemics, or other force majeure events, we may extend refund windows and provide additional support.
