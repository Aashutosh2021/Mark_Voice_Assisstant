Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 27, 2025

1. Data Collection Policy

MARK AI does not collect any personal data from you. All your interactions, voice commands, and personal information are stored locally on your computer only.

What Stays on Your Device

  • All voice commands and conversations with MARK AI
  • Your personal preferences and settings
  • Chat history and interaction logs
  • User configurations and customizations

Payment Information Only

  • Payment details are processed directly by Razorpay (our secure payment partner)
  • We only receive confirmation of successful payment, not your payment details
  • No financial information is stored by MARK AI

2. How Your Data Remains Private

Since we don't collect your data, here's how MARK AI protects your privacy:

  • All voice processing happens locally on your device
  • Your conversations never leave your computer
  • No personal data is transmitted to our servers
  • Software updates don't include any personal information
  • Customer support is provided without accessing your data
  • Your privacy is protected by design, not by policy alone

3. Local Data Security

Your data security is ensured through local storage:

  • All voice data is processed and stored only on your device
  • No personal information is transmitted to external servers
  • Your data is protected by your own computer's security
  • Payment processing is handled directly by Razorpay's secure systems
  • MARK AI cannot access your personal conversations or data

4. No Data Sharing

We cannot share what we don't have. Since MARK AI doesn't collect your personal data:

  • We have no personal information to sell, trade, or share
  • Your conversations and data remain exclusively on your device
  • No third parties have access to your MARK AI interactions
  • Government requests cannot access data we don't possess
  • Your privacy is protected by the absence of data collection

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information
  • Opt-out of certain data collection practices
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • File complaints with relevant data protection authorities

6. Voice Data Processing

MARK AI processes voice data completely locally on your device:

  • All voice processing occurs exclusively on your local device
  • No voice data is ever transmitted to external servers
  • Voice recordings are stored only on your computer
  • You have complete control over your voice data
  • No internet connection required for voice processing

7. Children's Privacy

MARK AI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of MARK AI after such modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
